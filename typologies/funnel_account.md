title: Funnel account

## Description

A funnel account is an individual or business account in one geographic area that receives multiple cash deposits, often in amounts below the cash reporting threshold, and from which the funds are withdrawn in a different geographic area with little time elapsing between the deposits and the withdrawals. The technique lets a criminal organisation move currency across the country without physically transporting it. Public advisories associate it with narcotics and smuggling proceeds and note that the withdrawn funds may then pay for goods in trade-based laundering schemes.

## Red-flag indicators

1. **FUN-01** Cash deposits made into the account at branches in states or cities far from where the account was opened and where the account holder lives or works
2. **FUN-02** Deposits made by individuals who are not account signers, often anonymous third parties who hold only the account number
3. **FUN-03** Funds withdrawn in a different geographic area shortly after deposit, by ATM withdrawal, wire or check, leaving a low balance
4. **FUN-04** Deposits kept below reporting or identification thresholds across many locations
5. **FUN-05** Account holder has no evident business or personal reason for deposit activity spread across the country
6. **FUN-06** Outgoing transfers from the account pay businesses unrelated to the account holder, for example exporters of goods

## What the narrative should establish

- Where the deposits were made relative to the branch where the account is held
- Who made the deposits, if known, and whether they are signers on the account
- How quickly and where the deposited funds were withdrawn or transferred
- Whether the account holder has any stated reason for out-of-area deposits
