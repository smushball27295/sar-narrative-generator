## Description

A money mule is a person who receives and moves illicit proceeds on behalf of others, knowingly or not. Mules are often recruited through job advertisements, social media or online relationships. Their personal accounts receive payments from fraud victims and the funds are quickly withdrawn in cash or forwarded by wire, remittance, person-to-person transfer or virtual currency, usually after the mule keeps a commission.

## Red-flag indicators

1. **MUL-01** A personal account, often newly opened, receives deposits or transfers from multiple unrelated individuals
2. **MUL-02** Funds are withdrawn in cash or forwarded shortly after receipt, by wire, person-to-person transfer or remittance
3. **MUL-03** The account holder retains a small portion of each incoming payment, consistent with a commission
4. **MUL-04** Account activity is inconsistent with the holder's age, occupation or income, for example a student or unemployed person handling large sums
5. **MUL-05** Incoming payments are later reported as fraudulent or unauthorised by the senders, or the sending institutions request recalls
6. **MUL-06** The account holder cannot explain the source of funds or says they are acting for an employer or online acquaintance

## What the narrative should establish

- Who the account holder is and how long the account has been open
- Who sent the funds, how many senders there were and whether they are related to the holder
- How and how quickly the funds left the account and what share was retained
- Any fraud reports, recall requests or explanations given by the account holder
